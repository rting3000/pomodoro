01 — About

A practice built on restraint.

Ten years ago I traded a career in industrial design for something slower. I now split my time between a small studio in Copenhagen and a workshop outside Kyoto, and most projects begin the same way — by asking what can be removed, not added.

The work that follows sits between two traditions that, to me, were always saying the same thing: hygge's honest materials, and wabi-sabi's comfort with the imperfect and unfinished.
